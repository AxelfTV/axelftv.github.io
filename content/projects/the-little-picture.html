THE LITTLE PICTURE

View on itch.

The Little Picture is a 3D platforming adventure created by a small team of passionate individuals. the game follows May, a young girl navigating dream worlds while reflecting on her parents’ separation.

The game features 3 vibrant environments to explore, clean and responsive movement mechanics, light puzzle-solving elements and a unique companion mechanic centered around Woof, May's stuffed sheep, who allows gliding, double jumps, and puzzle interactions.

My Role

I was a game designer and the lead programmer. My time was spent on developing the core systems of the game such as the character controller built using a finite state machine, the companion AI, level implementation, and mechanics design. I spent a large amount of time on polish and QA aswell.

Over the course of development I worked closely with the other members of my team to ensure a smooth development pipeline. This included the use of version control tools such as GitHub, and project management tools such as Asana.
